# Kuku AI Generalist — PromoIQ (Problem 1: Find the Promos)

**Problem framing.** A weekly microdrama release requires editors to rewatch episodes to find 20–60s shareable moments. I built a **screenplay-only, evidence-grounded shortlist tool**: it automates discovery and timestamp checking, while reserving creative acceptance for an editor. I picked this problem because it combines deterministic time constraints and subjective narrative judgment, making the boundary between code, AI and people explicit.

**Assumptions.** The supplied 50 text scripts contain scene-local episode timestamps and descriptions of final filmed moments. Timestamps are **approximate footage locations** until verified. A good promo has a supported hook in the first 3 seconds, an important outcome unresolved at its last frame, and an intelligible conflict without prior episodes. A valid tool may choose **no promo** for a weak episode. Stitches are permitted but require additional continuity review. No editing, music or overlays are in scope.

**Approach.** `parser.py` preserves dialogue/action/camera/sound with precise times; `scene_analyzer.py` indexes evidence; `hook_detector.py` performs broad candidate recall then grounded validation. `promo_generator.py` considers meaningful unresolved endings and duration-valid windows, avoiding mid-dialogue cuts. `editorial_guard.py` rejects unsupported camera-only hooks, generic questions masquerading as cliffhangers, and false rescue labels; other weak clips are marked `review`. Diversity filtering produces a per-episode shortlist and global ranking. Stitched two-/three-segment experiments are validated and prioritized separately. Optional **local Ollama** judges the full in-clip first 3 seconds/last 5 seconds on four dimensions, with at most 3 clip requests per episode by default; it was **not available** for the delivered run. Editors approve before posting.

**Real run.** All **50 scripts / 3,727 timecoded events** parsed; **694 hooks**, **139 cliffhanger anchors**, and **75 shortlisted continuous promos across 34 episodes**. The other **16 episodes deliberately have no credible automated candidate**. All selected clips passed structural QA; **7 stitched options** passed structural checks but not human continuity review. The source audit flags one uncorrected timestamp warning in Episode 022. **42 automated regression tests passed.** Runtime and its narrow scope are recorded in `outputs/execution_benchmark.json`. Rule-only mode made **0 paid model API calls**; editor time and optional local-LLM hardware cost are unknown.

**Weak selections / fixes.** An earlier Episode 047 cut mislabeled a plea for mercy as a rescue cliff; it is now rejected because the final seconds contain no physical rescue danger. A candle establishing shot was incorrectly allowed to count as an immediate hook; the new gate rejects it without a corroborating dramatic beat. Episode 005 kneeling to retrieve a book must not be interpreted as a romantic proposal (regression-tested). The Episode 003 punch-to-proposal candidate, **01:24.00–02:20.50**, survives and is the script-only first-post recommendation; the heuristic's numeric first place is Episode 028. Further cases and limitations are in `outputs/QUALITY_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md`.

**Checking, without inventing evidence.** Compared with naively taking each episode's first 40 seconds, PromoIQ best available selections have 29/34 cases passing all three **structural evidence** indicators, versus 1/50 for the baseline. This is an **expected selection-biased proxy gain, not measured creative quality**. An overlap-free V2 blind quality study is prepared (20 top, 10 low, 10 rejected; two empty rating sheets) and **awaits real editors**. The project never claims measured acceptance rate, video validation, click-through rate or conversion.

**Reproduce:** `python3 run_pipeline.py --all --auto-sample-gate --experimental-stitches --force`, `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v`, `python3 compare_baseline.py`, `python3 evaluate_quality.py score --set human_evaluation_v2` (only after two real independent reviews).
